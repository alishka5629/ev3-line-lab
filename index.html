Understand your robot.

Automatic: press Run. The assistant reads the program’s EV3 text prompts and button waits, positions the sensor and presses the requested buttons. It supports UP/DOWN/ENTER, repeated ENTER and other button orders. Programs without a calibration wait start from the right edge.

Startup instructions must be recognizable. Hidden conditions, image-only prompts, external inputs or unusual calibration routines may need Laboratory mode. If setup cannot be identified, the assistant stops with an explanation instead of reporting success.

Laboratory: open .ts or .uf2 and press Run. Follow your program’s instructions on the EV3 screen. For Basic/Advanced: On black → UP; On white → DOWN; On edge → ENTER. Wait one second after each sample. Some programs use ENTER for all three steps. LEFT is delivered to your program; Stop terminates execution.

Placement: Shift + click places the axle; Q/E rotate by 5°. Scroll to zoom and drag to move the camera. Calibrate the gyro while stationary.

Speaker: Run enables browser audio. Standard MakeCode tones, built-in effects and embedded WAV sounds play through the robot speaker. Enable audio retries browser permission; Mute and Volume affect listening, while program timing is preserved. Stop, Reset and hiding the tab stop playback. A recording must be embedded or passed to a supported MakeCode audio API; a filename in a comment is not audio.

UF2: embedded MakeCode source is recovered and recompiled. Native firmware without source is unsupported. main.ts runs with standard EV3 libraries; extensions and multi-file projects may require changes.

Accuracy: configurable differential-drive physics, encoders, sensor footprint, noise and slip. Real geometry and traction vary. A single sensor cannot resolve arbitrary junctions without a route policy. Lab Bonus and Baku City Circuit are approximate layouts.
