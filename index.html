Simulator help

Automatic. Choose a track and program, then press Run. The simulator follows recognized EV3 startup prompts.

Laboratory. Press Run, follow the EV3 screen and use the placement and brick buttons. Lab Bonus V3 Solo uses ENTER on black, floor and the right edge.

Camera. Scroll to zoom. Drag the view to move the camera. Shift-click places the axle and Q/E rotates the robot.

Programs. Open a .ts file or a .uf2 with embedded MakeCode source. Use Save to download the editor contents.

Robot speaker. Expand the speaker controls to adjust volume or mute supported MakeCode sounds.
